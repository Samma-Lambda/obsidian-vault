###### One of SUMRY's primary goals is to build an inclusive community over the summer. Reflect on your membership in a community to which you feel connected. Why is this community meaningful to you? You may define community however you like. (please limit your response to 1 paragraph)
The community I feel most connected to is the mathematics community at Willamette. Although it may sound cliché on a math REU application, this community inspired my love of mathematics. 
As a freshman in my first proof-based course, I often went to the math tutors to make sure my proofs were clear and readable, and quickly became friends with those in the math department. They showed me that math didn't need to be competitive or isolated, but instead as a shared mental frolic. Math problems weren't a chore, but instead a mental mountain you get to climb with your friends and absurd amounts of caffeine. Wether talking about strange math questions late at night, or playing board games at math club this community has been such an amazing part of my life. I hope to give underclassmen and those around me a similar experience.
