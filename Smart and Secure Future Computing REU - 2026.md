##### Personal Statement 
I am a third-year student at Willamette University pursuing degrees in Computer Science, Data Science, and Mathematics. After completing my undergraduate studies, I plan to pursue a PhD in applied math, data science, or computer vision. Because Willamette is a low-research institution, I am applying to this program both to gain the experience needed to be a competitive graduate applicant and because I would genuinely love to spend my summer studying machine learning, data science and education. 

While I have always been interested in data science, attending Willamette to specifically study this topic, I discovered how much I truly enjoyed data science when I joined a data science research role. Although I was interested in my course work, working on this project showed me how much enjoyed applying the skills I had developed in a real world setting. 

In this work, we study sustainable language in a variety of settings, including corporate communications, social media, and academic papers. To efficiently gather relevant text, I developed a machine learning classifier to automate part of the process. Building this system involved a significant learning curve, during which I gained experience with feature generation, training and validation splits, and the pro's and con's of different classification models. 

Beyond learning about machine learning through my job, I also gained experience within this topic in my computer vision course. Rather than focusing on specific machine learning architectures, the course emphasized sources of bias in machine learning and feature generation specifically for image data. Toward the end of the course, we studied neural networks and transformers in more detail. This was my favorite class that I have taken so far, it was so fun and has inspired me to potentially study this for my PhD research. 

Finally, beyond my interest in the machine learning, I am also strongly interested in education. During the summer after my freshman year, I conducted research on the effects of large language models on college level education, which resulted in a publication at SIGCITE 2025. In addition, I have been a teaching assistant for two years, supporting courses including Intro to Data Science, Data Structures, Analysis of Algorithms, and Database Management. After completing my PhD, I aim to become a professor, with a particular interest in teaching more mathematically oriented computer science courses such as probabilistic algorithms, or computer vision. I love the more mathematical side of computer science and hope that the courses I eventually teach will reflect this. 

Overall I hope to join this program to learn more about how machine learning is applied in the real world, to prepare for graduate school, and to spend the summer around people who love the same field I do.
