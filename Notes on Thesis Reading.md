This paper studies making graphs embedded in toruses and focuses on making them into planar visibility graphs. 
These embedded graphs are defined as a graph on some surface, with each edge being a strait line that does not intersect any other edges. They do not allow loops 
1. This algorithm works by taking every face(isolated section from the graph) and bounding it with a cycle 
2. Make sure no edge crosses the center line twice 
3. Make sure that the space it is in is a retangle 
4. Move all the points from a boundary 
5. The corner is not used
