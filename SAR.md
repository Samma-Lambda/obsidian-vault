# What is SAR(Synthetic Aperture Radar)
This is where you have a moving radio antenna and use the moment to increase the resolution of the reconstructed image. Using the movement we are able to utilize both multiple angles and the doppler effect.
The part that emits the radar pulse is known as the **Phase Center** and we model its position as 
$$
r_{a}(\tau)=[x_a(\tau),y_a(\tau),z_a(\tau)]^T
$$
