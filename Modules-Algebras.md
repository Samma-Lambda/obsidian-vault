# Modules
The easiest way to think of a module is a vector space with a ring as the scalars rather than a field. Beyond that a module does not have to have a basis, unlike a vector space. 
Examples include 
- The real numbers $\mathbb{R}$ with scalars from $\mathbb{Z}$


# Algebras
Algebras are modules that have a way to "multiply" vectors in a way that is bilinear.
Examples include 
- The cohomology with the cup product
